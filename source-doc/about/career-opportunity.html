Career opportunity

Veanora believes that talented and motivated people are essential to building a strong fashion brand. We aim to create career opportunities for individuals who are creative, responsible, customer-focused, and interested in growing within the clothing and retail industry.

Career opportunities may be available in areas such as fashion design, product development, merchandising, sourcing, quality control, photography, content creation, digital marketing, e-commerce operations, sales, customer support, warehousing, and delivery coordination.

We look for people who:

  • Have a genuine interest in fashion and lifestyle products
  • Communicate professionally and work well within a team
  • Understand the importance of customer satisfaction
  • Are willing to learn, improve, and accept responsibility
  • Can contribute creative ideas and practical solutions
  • Respect deadlines, quality standards, and workplace values

Apply now

If you believe your skills, experience, and commitment align with this opportunity, we encourage you to apply. Please send your updated CV, a brief introduction about yourself, and relevant work samples (if applicable) to our email address for consideration.
