Hours & Location

Veanora operates as a clothing and e-commerce brand serving customers in Bangladesh. Customers can explore collections and place online orders through our website at any time, subject to product availability and delivery coverage.

Customer service hours, showroom information, office location, holiday schedules, and special opening times will be updated through our official website and social media pages.

Customer support hours

Our support team responds to messages and inquiries during regular business hours. Response times may vary during weekends, public holidays, Eid campaigns, seasonal sales, and periods of high order volume.
