Certifications

Veanora is committed to maintaining appropriate business, trade, quality, and compliance documentation relevant to its operations in Bangladesh. We also encourage our suppliers and production partners to follow suitable standards for fabric quality, workmanship, product safety, and ethical business conduct.

Where applicable, verified certificates, registrations, testing documents, or compliance information may be displayed on our website or provided through authorized representatives.

Our approach to compliance includes:

  • Working with responsible suppliers and production partners
  • Reviewing product quality before customer delivery
  • Maintaining accurate business and customer records
  • Protecting customer information during online transactions
  • Following applicable e-commerce and consumer service practices
  • Updating documentation as the company expands
